KlenPOS ("the App") is a point-of-sale and order-management application for laundry and similar businesses, operated by Gona Janardhan Reddy, an individual (sole proprietor) based in Jammalamadugu, Andhra Pradesh, India ("we", "us"). Contact: itsreddygona@gmail.com.

The App is currently offered only in India.

## 1. Who is responsible for which data

KlenPOS is a business tool. Accounts are not open to the public: we create each business account ourselves after an in-person or direct agreement with the store owner.

- For the business's own customer and order data, the **store owner is the data fiduciary** (controller) and **we act as the data processor**: we store and process that data only to provide the App to them.
- For the login and account details of store owners and staff, and for app diagnostics, we are the data fiduciary.

## 2. What we collect

**Account data (store owners and staff):** name, phone number or email used to log in, role (owner or employee), and the business (store/outlet) name and details that the owner provides.

**Business data entered by the store:** customer names and phone numbers, orders and items, prices, invoices, payment records (amount, mode and status as recorded by the store), expenses, and staff records.

**Technical and diagnostic data:** crash reports and error logs (Firebase Crashlytics), usage events such as login, order placed, payment recorded and screen views (Firebase Analytics), device and app information (model, OS version, app version), an app installation identifier, and, when notifications are enabled, a push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging). Diagnostic and analytics data is linked only to opaque internal IDs, not to customer names or phone numbers.

**What we do not collect:** your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, or card/bank/UPI credentials. The App only records that a payment was made, as entered by the store. It does not process or touch payments.

## 3. Why we use it

- To provide the App: sign-in, orders, billing records, reports and offline sync.
- To keep the service secure and working: fix crashes, prevent abuse, and send service messages.
- To understand feature usage in aggregate and improve the App.
- To manage subscriptions and support.

We do not sell personal data and we do not show ads.

## 4. Where data is stored and who processes it

- **Our servers:** hosted on Vercel, with the database on Neon (PostgreSQL). [CONFIRM REGION of each, and update if outside India.]
- **Google Firebase** (Crashlytics, Analytics, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config) for diagnostics, analytics and notifications.
- **On your device:** the App keeps a local cache of recent data so it works offline. Sign-in tokens are kept in the operating system's secure storage.

Data may be processed outside India by these providers, under their own security and data-protection terms. We do not share data with anyone else except where required by law.

All data moves between the App and our servers over encrypted HTTPS connections.

## 5. How long we keep data

- **Active subscription:** data is kept while the account is active.
- **Subscription not renewed:** the account is placed on hold. Data is retained so the store can resume exactly where it left off once it subscribes again.
- **Account deletion requested:** store owners and employees can request deletion inside the app. Your data is then kept for a grace period (90 days) and **permanently deleted after it ends**. If you sign in and choose to restore your account before then, the deletion is cancelled and nothing is lost.
- Copies in provider backups are removed as those backups expire. We may keep limited records where the law requires (for example billing records).

**Deletion log.** To show that we honoured each request, we keep a minimal deletion log: an opaque internal ID, the role (owner or employee), and the request and completion dates. It contains no name, phone number or email address. ## 6. Your rights and account deletion

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data and withdraw consent, and you may nominate someone to act for you.

- **Store owners and staff:** email itsreddygona@gmail.com from your registered contact and we will act on the request within [30] days.
- **A store's customers:** your data is held by the store you used. Please ask the store; we will help the store fulfil it.
- **Delete my account / data:** request deletion in the app (owners delete the whole store and its data; employees delete their own login), or email itsreddygona@gmail.com with the subject "Delete account". Deletion follows Section 5.

Grievance contact: Gona Janardhan Reddy, itsreddygona@gmail.com.

## 7. Security

We use HTTPS, access controls and secure credential storage. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting you occurs we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.
